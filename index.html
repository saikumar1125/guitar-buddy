Play guitar in 7 simple steps

Sit & hold it

Sit up straight. Guitar body on your right thigh (left if left-handed), neck pointing slightly up, strumming forearm on top of the body.

Know the 6 strings

Thickest → thinnest: E · A · D · G · B · e. Trick: "Eddie Ate Dynamite, Good Bye Eddie".

Tune it

Open the Tuner tab. An out-of-tune guitar sounds bad even when you play right.

Press the strings right

Use the tip of each finger just behind the fret. Thumb behind the neck, fingers curved. Sore fingertips for 1–2 weeks is normal.

Learn first chords

Start with Em & Am, then C, G, D. Dots = press, ○ = open string, × = skip it.

Strum

Loose wrist, like shaking water off your hand. First down only, counting 1-2-3-4. Later: D, DU, UDU.

Change chords slowly

Switch Em ↔ Am for a minute while strumming in time. Keep going even if it's messy. 10 min every day beats 2 hours once a week.

    Tuning steps

    1. Press Start tuner and click Allow for the microphone.
    2. Pluck one open string (no fret pressed). Let it ring.
    3. Needle left = too low → tighten the peg a little.
    4. Needle right = too high → loosen a little.
    5. When it glows green, that string is done.
    6. Go thickest → thinnest: E, A, D, G, B, e, then re-check the first.

    Prefer tuning by ear? Tap a string button to hear the right note and match it.
